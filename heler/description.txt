App Name: PDFMasterPro

Short Description:
All-in-one PDF Tool: Editor, Converter, Scanner, OCR & More. Merge, Split & Compress.

Long Description:
Unlock the full potential of your documents with PDFMasterPro, the ultimate all-in-one PDF solution for your mobile device. Whether you need to edit, convert, organize, or secure your files, PDFMasterPro offers a professional suite of tools designed for speed and efficiency.

Key Features:

📄 Powerful PDF Converter
- Image to PDF: Convert your photos, scans, and images into high-quality PDF files instantly.
- PDF to Image: Extract pages from your PDFs and save them as high-resolution JPG images.

🛠 Advanced PDF Tools
- Merge PDF: Combine multiple PDF files into a single, organized document.
- Split PDF: Extract specific pages or split a large document into smaller files.
- Compress PDF: Reduce file size while maintaining quality for easy sharing.
- Organize Pages: Reorder, delete, or rotate pages within your PDF documents.
- Add Page Numbers: Automatically number your document pages for professional formatting.

📷 Scanner & OCR
- Smart Scanner: Use your camera to scan physical documents directly into PDF.
- OCR (Text Recognition): Extract text from images and scanned PDFs to make them editable and searchable.

🔒 Security & Privacy
- Protect PDF: Add passwords to encrypt and secure your confidential documents.
- Unlock PDF: Remove passwords from protected PDFs (owner password required).
- Watermark: Add custom text or images to your pages to protect your intellectual property.

Why PDFMasterPro?
- User-Friendly Interface: Clean, intuitive design makes complex PDF tasks simple.
- Fast & Reliable: Process files in seconds, right on your device.
- Offline Mode: Work on your documents anytime, anywhere without an internet connection.
