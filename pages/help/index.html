Help & User Guide

How likely is my data to leak?

Zero chance. PDF Master Pro runs 100% offline within your browser. Your files never leave your computer and are not uploaded to any server.

How do I merge PDF files?

Go to the Dashboard or Quick Tools, select "Merge PDF", drag and drop your files, arrange them in the desired order, and click "Merge".

Why are some OCR conversions taking long?

OCR is a computationally intensive task running in your browser. Large documents or high-resolution images can take more time to process.

Can I run the extension without internet?

Yes! All core features work offline. The only exception is the first-time download of OCR language packs.
